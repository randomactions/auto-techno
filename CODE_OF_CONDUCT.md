# Code of Conduct

Auto Techno is an open technical and musical collaboration. Participate with curiosity, precision, and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make listening feedback concrete and reproducible where possible.
- Respect different experience levels, tools, identities, and musical tastes.
- Keep public contributions free of private information, credentials, harassment, and unrelated promotional material.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, real-time safety, and the project's musical direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Reporting

For content that violates GitHub's policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting tools. For a repository-specific concern that must not be public, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/randomactions/auto-techno/security/advisories/new) and clearly label it as a conduct report rather than a security vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
