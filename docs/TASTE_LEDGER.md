# Taste ledger

## 2026-08-08 — Alien Analog V1 candidate

Observation: the Persistent V3 drum and phrase foundation is credible, but the
upper voices still reveal their primitive oscillator origins. Adding more
independent synth functions or a longer generic texture rack would increase
complexity without creating one recognizable authored instrument.

Rule: derive one `SynthPerformancePlan` from the scene seed and Scene DNA, then
render motif, upper interlock, atmosphere, response, and transition as roles of
one persistent Alien Analog topology. Establishment reveals the anchor, builds
corrode it, breakdowns retain processed residue, and returns restore attack.
Only the upper voice receives the 5-in-16 shadow, continuous seven-step accent,
and three-step echo clocks; kick, bass, primary hats, and clap remain identical.

Fixed-seed comparison set: `42`, `48291`, `90909`. Compare A (frozen Persistent
V3), B (Alien Analog with the existing rhythm), and C (Alien Analog plus the
cross-cycle plan) at matched RMS. B must beat A for authored identity, analog
movement, weight, and concealed oscillator character on two seeds; C must beat
B for hypnosis and syncopation without weakening the drums on two seeds.

Evidence: all three seeds pass deterministic structure, unchanged foundation
schedules, kick-safe interlocks, finite output, conservative peak, DC,
boundary, mono-low-end, multiple-partial, and evolving-centroid checks. The
release median improved from an initial `4.87×` to `2.37×` A after optimizing
only the voice math, but still fails the required `≤1.10×` gate. Human listening
is pending. This entry does not promote C or justify adding another effect
layer.

## 2026-07-13 — Persistent dark-hypnotic performance model

Observation: the approved v2 engine has weight and a broad effect vocabulary,
but bar-local reseeding makes variation feel forgetful. Musical material changes
without first becoming a recognizable idea, so added complexity can still read
as procedural and basic.

Rule: a scene now derives one immutable `SceneDNA` and an asymmetric 32-bar
`PerformancePlan`. Bars repeat or transform established rhythm and motif cells;
they never create identity by incrementing the scene seed. Foreground roles are
limited to four, bass steps are derived around kick anchors, and rare signature
events occur only at phrase boundaries.

Fixed-seed comparison set: `42`, `48291`, `90909`, plus failure probes `7` and
`77777`. Compare the previous approved v2 references with the new performance
model at matched integrated loudness. Listen specifically for recognizable
motif/groove memory, earned returns, restrained microvariation, kick/bass
authority, and whether a full scene becomes more compelling over time.

Implementation evidence: deterministic structural tests cover identity,
asymmetric phrase coverage, bounded transformations, foreground-role limits,
relational kick/bass placement, and rare signature-event frequency. Offline
quality reporting now includes gated integrated/short-term loudness, loudness
range, crest factor, three-band energy, centroid estimate, and transient density.
Subjective approval remains pending; this entry does not promote the new model
solely from automated checks.

### Second refinement — phrasing that reaches the renderer

Reassessment observation: the first persistent plan retained identity, but
several render gestures still assumed eight-bar phrases, swing offsets were
lost when DNA became render events, role truncation could discard atmosphere
and transition, and four of the five signature events were mostly telemetry.

Refined rule: phrase-local position now owns returns and fills; a shared bounded
accent contour shapes kick, bass, hats, and clap; eligible hats and bass retain
the scene's deterministic swing; structural roles are arbitrated instead of
truncated; and every signature event has an audible consequence. Scene DNA's
timbral family now selects one of four stable oscillator relationships, while
responses derive their placement and pitch from the established motif.

Use the same five fixed seeds. In the next matched-loudness comparison, listen
for phrase breathing without obvious velocity randomization, returns that land
at the actual phrase boundary, recognizable synth identity between sections,
and rare events that feel consequential rather than decorative.

## 2026-07-12 — First guided teaching session

The listener completed 16 guided choices in Teach Taste mode and ended the
session when the candidate sets felt consistently worth choosing from. No
cloud model or external account was involved; this is the local semantic
profile exported from the app's persisted `AutoTechno.tasteProfile` value.

### Learned direction

The current taste center is:

- dark but not maximally murky;
- strongly hypnotic;
- restrained in groove and syncopation rather than frantic;
- sparse and understated melodically;
- low in atmosphere/space;
- low-to-moderate aggression and chaos;
- moderate pace of change.

This should be treated as a starting prior, not a permanent style rule. Future
teaching sessions should be compared against this snapshot rather than
silently overwriting it.

## 2026-07-13 — Sequencer ambient first slice

Observation: alongside sustained drone ambient, the listener requested a
drum-locked sequenced atmosphere in the direction of JakoJako and State Azure.
The layer should feel like a repeating musical mechanism rather than a second
free-running melody, and should be available without replacing the techno
groove or drone.

Rule: `Sequencer ambient` is an optional semantic layer whose events are chosen
from hat/kick-safe steps and remain deterministic for a scene seed. Its shared
mechanism exposes pulse-network, arpeggiated-motif, and textural-step-field
variants; density, register, repetition, drift, and depth shape the result.

Fixed-seed comparison set: `42`, `48291`, `90909`. Structural tests verify
determinism, drum locking, distinct variants, and independence from drone
level. Listening comparison remains required before further taste tuning.

## 2026-07-13 — Enhanced drones first slice

Observation: the requested bvdub / Biosphere-adjacent space should feel like
an evolving presence that can remain after the source tone recedes, rather
than a short decorative room effect.

Rule: `Drone` drives a persistent phase-continuous harmonic bed that gradually
leans toward filtered noise and sends into bounded phrase-scale reverb memory.
The drone is strongest in breakdowns and transitions, ducks around kick/bass,
and never owns the low-end center.

Fixed-seed comparison set: `42`, `48291`, `90909`. Offline checks cover
determinism, finite bounded output, stereo decorrelation, and tail persistence.

## 2026-08-08 — 96-bar dramatic debt/payoff proof

Observation: the desired level-up is professional authorship and earned
long-form tension/release, not compatibility with Serum or any other synth.
The existing 32-bar forms and scalar tension model cannot represent what was
withheld, why anticipation exists, or exactly what a return must pay back.

Rule: compile a 96-bar score with independent tension dimensions and explicit
musical debts. The first proof makes bar 24 an intentionally incomplete return,
withholds foundation, downbeat certainty, spectral relief, spatial proximity,
and motif completion across the later arc, pays the main debt at bar 80, and
resolves the motif at bar 84. The same score A/Bs the legacy motif voice against
one authored internal Shadow Pressure patch with persistent state.

Fixed-seed comparison set: `42`, `48291`, `90909`. The offline report passes
determinism, debt/payoff closure, finite output, true-peak, low-band stereo, and
boundary checks. Across all three seeds, the bar-80 return RMS is more than four
times bar 79. Human listening remains the gate: the return must feel earned and
the authored voice must sound clearly preferable, not merely different.

## 2026-08-08 — The interface stops composing

Observation: the previous control-heavy app was laggy and kept musical
authority in the UI. The desired product is an autonomous instrument: listen,
pause, resume, and let the engine own every other decision.

Rule: ship one Play/Pause control at a fixed 130 BPM. Former semantic controls
remain correlated internal composition dimensions, not observable app state.
The sole runtime is the deterministic Persistent V3 director; MVP, approved-v2,
treatment, mastering, effect, fixed-seed, and taste-teaching variants remain
offline reference tools only.

Performance rule: prepare current and next 32-bar scenes away from the audio
callback, keep sample-time lookahead scheduling, and derive the 64-bucket
waveform during preparation. UI updates only the playhead at 15 Hz. If a future
visual requires a node tap, callback allocation, FFT, or continuous high-rate
telemetry, it does not belong in the shipped window.

## 2026-08-08 — Autonomous architecture integration checkpoint

Technical observation, not a listening verdict: a fixed 32-bar scene boundary
cannot carry actual phrase history, dramatic promises, or effect continuation
with enough precision for an unattended session. The integrated runtime now
prepares deterministic 4-16 bar phrases, passes voice and effect state across
their real boundaries, and freezes the last coherent phrase if preparation is
late. Symbolic stagnation and offline audio movement are evaluated before a
candidate reaches playback; graph generation is bounded to repository-owned
upper-voice processors while kick, bass, masking protection, glue, limiting,
and output routing remain protected.

Rule: do not derive a new taste rule from technical novelty alone. The first
post-integration human listening observation must name one audible moment, seed,
phrase/bar, and winner/loser comparison; that observation will drive the next
small deterministic rule. Initial runtime integration is judged by technical
validation and the hardware soak, not by a fabricated prior listening approval.
