# Video Analysis Protocol

Use this protocol whenever a production lesson, interview, performance, or its
comments motivate an Auto Techno hypothesis. The goal is bounded, auditable
input to the automated quality process—not authority, taste polling, or a manual
promotion verdict.

## Capture the source

- Record the video URL, title, creator, access date, and any relevant timestamp.
- For an active autonomous roadmap item, encode that provenance in its checked
  `docs/local/reports/source-citations/AT-xxxx.json` record under the
  [`SOURCE_CITATION_SCHEMA.md`](SOURCE_CITATION_SCHEMA.md) contract.
- Inspect the linked moment and its surrounding chapter or context. Do not
  generalize from an isolated sentence when the nearby demonstration qualifies
  it.
- Prefer a manual transcript. When only automatic captions are available, label
  them as automatic and treat uncertain terminology cautiously.
- Paraphrase the relevant claim with timestamps. Keep creator instruction
  separate from what the audio demonstration appears to show.

## Sample community evidence

- Sample up to 50 top-ranked top-level comments. Record the access date, sort
  order, number inspected, and any retrieval or caption limitation.
- Inspect up to five technically substantive threads, with no more than three
  replies from each. A reply is context or correction, not another independent
  vote from the top-level author.
- Do not retain usernames or reproduce whole comments. Paraphrase only the
  technical or perceptual point relevant to the engine.
- Classify observations as repeated convergence, specific dissent or technical
  correction, aesthetic sentiment, or non-actionable praise/workflow/gear talk.
- Require three independent comments expressing materially the same point before
  calling it convergence. Likes and repeated replies within one thread do not
  satisfy that threshold.
- A concrete technical correction may become a testable caution without
  convergence. It is not established fact until independently verified.

## Translate evidence into an engine hypothesis

- Keep creator claims, community convergence, dissent, direct human observation,
  and machine measurements as separate evidence classes.
- State one falsifiable deficit at a named canonical checkpoint: what engine
  responsibility is deficient, what evidence should expose it, and which
  observable result would disconfirm the hypothesis.
- Identify the existing canonical owner, reusable state or DSP capability,
  bounded action, continuation behavior, home correction or neutral ineligible
  behavior, and duplicate path avoided.
- Reject advice that requires a new user control, selectable seed, alternative
  engine, unrelated gear emulation, or extra density without a measurable
  product benefit.
- Treat spectral, loudness, waveform, comment, and creator evidence as
  descriptive until a versioned quality-policy rule defines a validated range or
  relationship.
- Record the source and hypothesis in the change's automated quality evidence.
  Preserve completed historical studies in `history/TASTE_EXPERIMENTS.md`; do
  not turn that archive back into an active approval ledger.

## Choose the causal engine shape

For each source, choose the implementation shape that expresses the durable
idea most directly. Reusing an existing score owner, voice, or effect is not a
goal by itself, and neither is adding a novel mechanism.

- Extend an existing mechanism when the source changes the behavior or
  relationship of material the canonical score already owns and the existing
  path can retain truthful score-to-PCM evidence.
- Add a new internal track, instrument, synthesis primitive, or effect chain
  when the source describes a genuinely missing role or signal process and the
  new owner is the smallest way to make the intended consequence attributable.
- Reject both reuse and novelty when they would encode workstation workflow,
  add density without a musical responsibility, duplicate an existing owner,
  or produce a parameter whose PCM and quality consequence cannot be measured.
- Compare the plausible shapes in the source design record and state why the
  selected one best fits the musical cause, bounds, continuation, fallback,
  realtime contract, and automated quality deficit.

While a video-improvement goal is active and the user has delegated design
judgment, this choice belongs to the implementation loop. It does not require a
separate per-video approval gate; deterministic qualification and exact-head
validation remain mandatory before publication.

## Qualification boundary

Videos, comments, view counts, creator authority, external measurements, and
human listening cannot promote or reject an engine revision by themselves. A
revision is promoted only by the deterministic automated qualification and
runtime-safety gates in `SOUND_QUALITY.md` and
`AUTONOMOUS_RUNTIME_VALIDATION.md`. Optional human feedback may start another
measurable hypothesis, but it is never a required curation gate.
